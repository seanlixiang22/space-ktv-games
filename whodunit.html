      怎么玩How to play

      📜 三条铁律

      1. 每一行只能有 1 个人，每一列也只能有 1 个人（像数独一样）。放下一个人后，TA 所在的整行、整列都不能再放别人——棋盘会自动把这些格子变暗。
      2. 🪴盆栽、🍾酒桌、🔊音箱上不能站人；🛋️沙发、🪑高脚凳、🎤麦克风位可以。
      3. 凶手是唯一和死者在同一个房间的人。

      🔍 线索里的说法

      紧挨着××：TA 上、下、左、右相邻的格子里，至少一格有××；隔着墙、在不同房间也算，斜角不算。
      斜对角挨着：两人的格子刚好斜着相邻（左上、右上、左下或右下那一格），不在同一个房间也算。
      更靠上 / 更靠左：所在的行号（列号）更小，不要求相邻。
      在窗边：TA 的格子边上画着蓝色窗户。

      🧠 推理工具

      🧍 放置：选人 → 点格子，确定位置。
      ✏️ 候选：选人 → 点格子，在格子里写上 TA 的缩写，表示"可能在这"。比如 "翔哥坐在沙发上"，就在所有沙发上标“翔”。
      你给某人只标了一个候选格时，那一格会闪绿光，放置模式下直接点它就能落子。
      💡 提示：只给文字线索，告诉你该从谁、从哪条线索入手；同一个人连续求提示，会一步步说得更具体，但不会替你放。
      ✕ 排除：在确定没人的格子打叉。
      点空白处或按 Esc 可以取消选中人物。不选人时，点格子里的某个候选：候选模式下删掉那一个，放置模式下把那个人放上去。放下或拿起一个人后不会自动选中别人。选中某人时，TA 的候选会高亮、别人的会变淡。
      放好一个人后，同行同列会自动变暗，相关候选自动清掉。

      ⌨️ 快捷键

      1–6 选人　QWE 切换模式　Ctrl+Z 撤销

      ⭐ 评分

      不用提示、一次答对 = 三星。

      📜 Three rules

      1. Only 1 person per row, and only 1 person per column (just like sudoku). Once someone is placed, nobody else can go in that row or column — the board dims those squares for you.
      2. Nobody can stand on a 🪴 plant, 🍾 drinks table or 🔊 speaker; a 🛋️ sofa, 🪑 bar stool or 🎤 mic stand is fine.
      3. The killer is the only person in the same room as the victim.

      🔍 What the clues mean

      Next to a ___: at least one of the squares directly above, below, left or right of them has it; it still counts through a wall, in a different room, but diagonals don't.
      Diagonally adjacent: their squares touch corner to corner (up-left, up-right, down-left or down-right), even if they are in different rooms.
      Row is above / column is left of: a smaller row (column) number; they don't have to be neighbours.
      By a window: a blue window is drawn on the edge of their square.

      🧠 Tools

      🧍 Place: pick a person, then tap a square to put them there.
      ✏️ Note: pick a person, then tap squares to pencil in their initials as "could be here". For "Brother Xiang is sitting on a sofa", mark X on every sofa.
      When someone has only one noted square left, it glows green; in Place mode just tap it.
      💡 Hint: text only — it tells you who and which clue to start from, and gets more specific if you keep asking about the same person. It never places anyone for you.
      ✕ Rule out: cross off squares you know are empty.
      Tap empty space or press Esc to deselect. With nobody picked, tap a single note in a square: in Note mode it deletes that note, in Place mode it places that person there. Placing or picking someone up never auto-selects another person. The picked person's notes are highlighted and everyone else's fade.
      After you place someone, their row and column dim and related notes are cleared.
